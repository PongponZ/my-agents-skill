ORANGE ORB WALKER - scene notes (evidence boundaries)
=====================================================
Source: references/primary_reference.png (R01, sole identity source). concept_sheet.png used only as a construction aid.
Scene convention: D = 1 scene unit (body diameter), NOT metres. +Z up, front -Y, support plane Z=0.
OOW_ROOT = ground projection of chassis centre. Body centre height H = 1.00 (chosen to follow foot contacts of the symmetric stance).

HANDEDNESS DECISION
  The brief's proposed convention said "+X = robot right" and "source-left C09 on +X". With front = -Y and +Z up,
  a front view shows +X on IMAGE RIGHT, so following that literally would mirror the tools against R01.
  R01 outranks the proposed convention, so robot-right parts (C09, C11_RF, C11_RR) are on -X and robot-left parts
  (C10, C11_LF, C12_LR_PROPOSED) are on +X. The FRONT view therefore matches R01 (C09 image-left, C10 image-right).
  The RIGHT inspection camera follows the brief's camera spec literally (from +X looking -X, = Blender Right view),
  which under this handedness shows the robot-LEFT side.

PROPOSED / INFERRED (custom property oow_evidence on objects)
  C12_LR_PROPOSED  - fourth walking leg; proposal only. R01 shows three clear blade tips; original leg count unresolved.
  Leg layout - SYMMETRIC by user request (follow the concept sheet): front legs at azimuth +-54 deg, rear legs at
    +-126 deg, identical reach; leg profile (out from the knee, then down, near-vertical blade) after the concept
    front/rear views and the C11 detail. This replaces the earlier R01-derived asymmetric stance, so the REF_MATCH
    view no longer reproduces R01's exact (asymmetric) blade-tip positions. Quadrant assignment is an editable
    construction convention, not proof of attachment locations.
  Leg detail (rev 3) - faceted ridged armour plates with seams, bolt heads/holes, latch, joint block, stepped knee
    and ankle discs, exposed dark side plates with warm dots, faceted wedge blade with keel inset, heavier edge wear
    (object property oow_wear = 1). Detail placement is mirrored so all four legs are mirror images.
  Body detail (rev 4) - top band tapers toward the ring (wedge, per brief C03); crown latitude seam and full
    meridian seams (21 armour panels); lower orange plates raised 0.010 as layered armour; recessed slots with raised
    dark bezels (front, side, lower holes, rear vent - rear vent inferred); sparse rivets; wider C04 surround;
    dark skirt lip under the armour rim (inferred); heavier painted wear and orange flecks on the ivory.
  C04/C05 - axis moved to the body centre (Z_RING = 0) by user request; layered bezel and ~0.09 recess follow the
    concept C04+C05 detail (generated aid, not measured).
  C13_*            - rear/underside armour and C13_UnderPlate: inferred, conservative continuation (ivory upper, orange lower,
    dark underside). No rear core, no rear roof emitter, no weapons/cables.
  Body fore-aft depth - inferred near-spherical (C01_Chassis oow_depth_scale_y = 1.0); not measured.
  C06 hidden rear surfaces - simple, non-emissive (inferred). C07_Neck hidden attachment - inferred.
  C09_Mount / C10_Mount - concealed grip geometry, minimal and inferred.
  All pivots (hip empties, *_Knee, *_Ankle, C08_*_Elbow, C08_*_Wrist) - visual pivot conventions, not a rig;
    no range of motion certified.
  Materials - dielectric paint by default (metallic 0); substrate unproven; colours are approximate, not calibrated.
  Physical scale - unknown.
